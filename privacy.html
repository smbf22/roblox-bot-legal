Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This service lets a visitor look up a public Roblox profile and share the displayed profile details with a configured Discord channel only after explicitly confirming.

The shared information may include Roblox username, display name, user ID, profile URL, and the time consent was recorded.

This service does not request or intentionally collect Roblox passwords, authentication cookies, or login credentials.

Shared profile details are sent to the Discord channel configured by the bot owner. People with access to that channel may see them.

Generated links expire after 30 minutes. Temporary link state is stored in memory and is lost when the service restarts. The bot does not maintain a separate database of profile submissions.

The hosting provider and Discord may process technical data according to their own policies. Contact the Discord server owner for questions or deletion requests concerning a submitted profile message.
